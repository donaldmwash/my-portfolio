Delivery, returns and FAQ

Delivery

We deliver countrywide. Nairobi orders usually arrive within 1 to 2 days and other towns within 2 to 4 days through courier or bus parcel. The delivery fee depends on your town and is confirmed on WhatsApp before you pay.

Payment

Pay by M-Pesa or on delivery in selected areas. Ritah confirms the payment details when she receives your order.

Returns

If your item arrives damaged or wrong, message us within 48 hours with a photo and we will replace it. For hygiene, opened makeup and skincare cannot be returned.

Privacy

We use your name, phone number and address only to process and deliver your order. We never sell your details.

Terms

Prices are in Kenyan shillings. Orders are confirmed once Ritah replies on WhatsApp. Stock is limited and shades may sell out.

Beauty tips

Match your foundation

Test the shade on your jawline in daylight. The right one disappears into your neck. Use our shade finder if you are unsure.

Skincare order

Cleanse, apply serum, then moisturiser with SPF in the morning. Give each layer a minute to settle.

Blush that lasts

Dab cream blush on the apples of your cheeks with your fingers and blend upward for a natural flush.
