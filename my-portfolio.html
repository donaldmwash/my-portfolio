How I work

From problem to working product.

I aim to make the reasoning visible: understand the need, shape the system, build the experience and verify the result.

STEP 01

Understand

Clarify the user, workflow, constraints and what success should look like.

STEP 02

Design the system

Plan the interface, data model, API boundaries and access rules before adding complexity.

STEP 03

Build & iterate

Implement in small, understandable pieces and improve based on real feedback.

STEP 04

Verify

Run checks, test important flows and document setup, trade-offs and next steps.
